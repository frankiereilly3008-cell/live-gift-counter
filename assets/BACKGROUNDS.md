# Scenic backgrounds

All images are bundled locally. The website does not fetch backgrounds from an outside service. Their source paths are relative to `theme-scenery.css`.

- `christmas-village.png`: the user's supplied snowy Christmas village image, copied without visual changes.
- `halloween-village.png`: generated with the built-in ImageGen tool for this project.
- `coastal-sunset.png`: generated with the built-in ImageGen tool for this project.

## Halloween generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: scenic full-screen website background, wide 16:9 landscape, cinematic detailed environmental image with no text or UI. Primary request: a beautiful atmospheric Halloween village at blue-purple twilight, looking straight down a cobbled pedestrian street into the distance, timber cottages on both sides, glowing amber windows, warm lanterns, carved jack-o-lanterns at doorsteps, bare twisting trees and drifting low mist, full moon in indigo sky, a few autumn leaves in foreground. A cozy spooky storybook photography feel, richly textured architecture, warm window lights against cool blue violet night. Central street is open, calm and slightly darker so website text can be legible. No humans close up, no monsters, no lettering, no watermark. High production quality, believable depth, soft depth of field with subtle amber bokeh, painterly photographic realism. Wide composition with readable scenery at the edges; not a poster, not an interface.

## Coastal generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: scenic full-screen website backdrop, wide 16:9 landscape, no text, no UI. Primary request: a peaceful beautiful coastal surf village at sunset, photographed from a wooden pedestrian boardwalk leading into a small seaside harbor and open ocean, warm lanterns and beach cottages at the sides, a few palms, gently rolling turquoise waves catching peach and gold sunlight, soft sea mist, distant headland, rich blue ocean below a coral-pink and indigo sky. Match the atmospheric cinematic photographic realism and cozy illuminated village mood of a scenic seasonal website. Open calm central path and broad sky/ocean negative space behind website text, scenery concentrated at edges, deep inviting perspective, realistic water reflections and weathered wood, subtle natural bokeh and depth of field. No people close up, no readable signs, no lettering, no logos, no watermark. Premium realistic landscape, not cartoon, not a poster. Tasteful saturated teal water and warm sunset, no overly bright white areas in center.
